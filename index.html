    Why stages work

    An empty tank is dead weight. Every kilo you keep carrying makes the engine work harder.

    Dropping it means the next engine only pushes what is left, so the same fuel gives much more speed (Δv).

    Solid boosters add a big kick at liftoff. Once lit they can't be throttled or switched off, and they drop away when empty.

    Upper stages fly in thin air, so they need less push and fins matter less. The autopilot steers with the engine.
